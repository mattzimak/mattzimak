# Matt Zimak

Founder of [Agentmatik](https://agentmatik.ai) (AI agents and automated workflows for SMEs and executives), co-founder and CEO of [Deep Noise](https://deepnoise.ai) (a generative audio engine that produces fully editable sound), co-founder and COO of [Museverse](https://museverse.xyz) (XR music education, live on the Meta Horizon Store). Prague and New York.

## Now

- Running three companies on git-based company brains: plain Markdown repos every agent reads through one `AGENTS.md` contract, fed by a meeting-to-brain pipeline and curated nightly, with sensitive changes waiting for a human in Slack.
- A fleet of scheduled Claude Code cloud agents pushes grants, content and side projects forward without me in the loop.
- Building at the YC "Own Your Intelligence" hackathon on 2026-09-27: a piano coach you own, trained on labels a deterministic music scorer hands out for free, with the learner's memory in a brain they control.
